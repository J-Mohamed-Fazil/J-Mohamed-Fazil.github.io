03 / WORKING METHODOLOGY

Analytical Approach & Framework

A disciplined four-phase analytical lifecycle ensuring data integrity, quantitative rigor, and actionable business impact.

PHASE 01

Problem Framing & Objectives

Isolate the strategic business question before writing queries. Define critical KPIs (SLA adherence, gross GMV, turnover multiplier), stakeholder constraints, and baseline targets.

PHASE 02

Data Auditing & ETL

Audit schemas for nulls, duplicates, and orphan records. Normalize tables into 3NF or dimensional Star Schemas (fact & dimension tables) using Power Query and SQL scripts.

PHASE 03

Quantitative Modeling

Author structured SQL CTEs, window functions (DENSE_RANK), reusable DAX measures, and Python statistical tests (IQR anomaly filtering, Mann-Whitney U hypothesis validation).

PHASE 04

Executive Synthesis

Synthesize findings into clean, decision-ready dashboards with dynamic slicers. Translate statistical discoveries into concrete, operational business recommendations.
